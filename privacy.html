Privacy policy

QuranCasts publishes Quran recitation videos with translations on its own YouTube channels, listed on the home page. This policy covers this website and the tool we use to manage those channels.

This website

This website is a set of static pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no advertising and no forms, and it collects no personal data. Links to our channels open YouTube, which is governed by Google's own policies.

Our YouTube API tool

We use YouTube API Services through a private script that only the channel owner runs on their own computer. It uploads our videos and sets their titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists and visibility on our own channels only.

  • It is not offered to the public and has no other users.
  • It does not collect, store or share any data about viewers or any other YouTube user.
  • The authorization tokens for our channels are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared.
  • The authorization can be revoked at any time at Google security settings.

By using YouTube you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. How Google handles data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.
